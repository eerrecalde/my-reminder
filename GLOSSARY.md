# My Reminders

Language for reminders, medicines, scheduled occurrences, and outcomes.

**Reminder**:
An item with a name and schedule that prompts a person at its scheduled times.

**Medicine**:
A reminder with optional dosage instructions. The app records these instructions without recommending a dose.

## Language

**Reminder occurrence**:
A single scheduled instance of a Reminder or Medicine at a particular date and time.

**Missed occurrence**:
An unanswered occurrence whose next occurrence has arrived. This does not establish whether a person took a medicine.

**Dose occurrence**:
A single scheduled dose of a medicine at a particular date and time. A dose already due remains the same occurrence when the medicine's future schedule changes.

**Unconfirmed dose**:
A dose occurrence that is due or past due with neither a taken nor a skipped outcome recorded. An unconfirmed dose does not establish whether the person actually took the medicine.
_Avoid_: Forgotten dose

**Paused medicine**:
A medicine whose reminders are suspended until resumed. Pausing preserves doses already due; resuming continues at the next future dose without catch-up reminders.

**Alarm screen**:
A dedicated view for a due reminder occurrence in Alarm mode. It can appear over the lock screen while the phone remains locked.

**Sound session**:
A shared period of alarm sound and vibration for overlapping reminder occurrences. Sound continues while an active alarm remains, until stopped or superseded. Stopping the sound does not record an outcome.
