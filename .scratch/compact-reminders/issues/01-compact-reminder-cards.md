# 01: Compact reminder cards

**What to build:** Replace the oversized reminder cards with the selected Soft cards design, keeping details visible and actions easy to use.

**Blocked by:** None (can start immediately).

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Reminder and Medicine cards show the name, readable schedule, and any medicine instructions, with wrapping for long content and schedules.
- [ ] Labeled edit, pause/resume and red delete icon actions are visible, with at least 48 × 48 dp targets and accessible names; delete still requires confirmation.
- [ ] Paused reminders are labeled and appear after active reminders; ordering within each group is stable.
- [ ] Existing editing, pausing, resuming, deletion and preservation of due occurrences work in English and Spanish.
- [ ] Application-level tests cover observable behavior and actions; verify the selected visual design, long text, larger text and touch targets on a disposable emulator.
- [ ] Follow the feature spec and its Soft cards visual reference.

**Visual reference:** [Selected Soft cards design](../soft-cards-reference.jpg). The feature specification defines the behavior and accessibility requirements.
