# 09: Schedule doses on selected weekdays

**What to build:** Add or edit a medicine that repeats on selected weekdays at one or more times, with the same review, reminders, and dose actions as a daily medicine.

**Blocked by:** 08 — Set treatment dates and preview the final reminder

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Select one or more weekdays and times, including a single weekday for a weekly schedule.
- [ ] Review the next three eligible reminders and, if the treatment has an end date, its final reminder.
- [ ] Verify reminders follow the phone’s local time zone after clock or zone changes. A nonexistent clock time moves forward by the clock-change gap, and a repeated clock time fires once at its first occurrence.
- [ ] Verify the weekday controls and review in both languages with large text and TalkBack.
